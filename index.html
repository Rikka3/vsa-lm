Causal Mechanics · Scroll-Driven Walkthrough

How Bolt Attention Works

Five continuous scroll chapters showing state allocation, GQA un-tying, centroid shadow cancellation, hybrid healing, and exact algebraic unlearning—rendered inside resolution-locked 4:3 viewports.

01 · KV WALL
Chapter 01 · Step 01

Standard Transformers Hoard Every Token

Every Softmax layer appends a full key and value vector per generated token. Long <think> reasoning chains and multi-file code contexts expand KV VRAM linearly with sequence length.

Chapter 01 · Step 02

9.2 GB of KV Cache for One Stream

At 65,536 tokens, Qwen/Qwen3-4B requires 9,216 MB of KV cache (1,792 MB on 1.5B) before activations or batching—starving consumer GPUs.

Chapter 01 · Step 03

Memory-Bandwidth Bottleneck

Every single decode step must re-stream the entire growing KV pile from HBM. Replacing 72–75% of layers with fixed-size D×D states cuts memory traffic sharply and raises 64k throughput 1.42×–1.57×.

Chapter 02 · Step 01

QK-Norm & Mix-First GQA Un-Tying

Donors with HKV=2 or 8 would only get 2 or 8 shared matrices if tied. Bolt Attention preserves donor QK-Norm, then mixes repeated KV heads via learned W_mix before applying RoPE—yielding H independent subspaces with zero phase drift past training length.

Chapter 02 · Step 02

Tensor Product Role-Filler Binding

Normalized features φ(z) = z + α·ReLU(z) bind key and value vectors via outer product k_t ⊗ v'_t into a fixed 128×128 associative matrix per head.

k~_t = RoPE_t(repeat_kv(k_raw)_h · W^mix_k) + Conv1D_4(k~)
Chapter 02 · Step 03

Learnable Multi-Scale Decay (∂L / ∂γ_h)

Each head learns its own decay horizon γ_h = 0.9999·σ(ℓ_h) using exact analytical cross-chunk gradients inside our custom Triton backward kernel—never frozen ALiBi slopes.

M_t = γ_h · M_(t-1) + k_t ⊗ v'_t
Chapter 02 · Step 04

Fused O(1) Triton Step & Split-KV Graph

Readout scales by steady-state (1 - γ_h)/√D inside a single fused Triton kernel captured inside a Split-KV CUDA Graph—constant O(1) compute per VSA layer at any context length.

02 · BOLT RECURRENCE
03 · UNBIND + SLOTS
Chapter 03 · Step 01

Why Raw Linear Memory Blurs Long CoT

Over 800+ <think> tokens, shared domain and syntax vectors accumulate inside M_t. Naive M_t q_t retrieves a heavy mean-field background floor, and RMSNorm_out amplifies residual noise.

Chapter 03 · Step 02

Centered Holographic Unbinding

Track tiny O(D) running centroids (k̄_t, v̄_t, z_t) (+1.5% state) and subtract the exact rank-1 background hologram before normalization via zero-init gate c_t = tanh(W_c x_t)—staying 100% Abelian linear.

y_clean = ((1-γ)/√D) · [ M_t q_t - c_t · ((k̄_t·q_t)/(z_t+ε)) · v̄_t ]
Chapter 03 · Step 03

Abelian Page-Slot Binder (No-RoPE)

Paired with fast M_t, non-decaying position-free slots (C_t, U_t, Z_t) routed by w_t = softmax(r)·σ(r) stay near zero on filler—preserving early prompt numbers across 32k+ tokens with 100% train/decode parity.

Chapter 04 · Step 01

26 Bolt-VSA (SWA=256) + 10 Softmax Anchors

Keeping L0 pinned + every 4th layer as full Softmax preserves initial token binding and long-range verification, while widening VSA local Softmax to W=256 keeps full docstrings and arithmetic steps lossless.

Chapter 04 · Step 02

Step-0 Stability + 2-Stage Healing

Initializing out_norm=0.05 prevents early-layer RMS amplification (dropping Step-0 PPL from 443.76 → 4.66), followed by Stage-1 attention transfer and Stage-2 T=2048 KD with unfrozen MLPs—reaching 0.981× donor PPL and 6/7 Inspect ties.

04 · HYBRID RETROFIT
05 · ERASE + STREAM
Chapter 05 · Step 01

Mountable .vsa Disk Cartridges

Documents encode into portable .vsa neural state files on SSD (0 MB idle RAM). Queries route in <75 ms, superpose cleanly onto live state, and recall facts at 100% (4/4) accuracy.

Chapter 05 · Step 02

Exact Algebraic Unlearning (0.00 Residual)

Because both M_t and centroids (k̄_t, v̄_t, z_t) obey linear superposition, subtracting decayed document tensors or calling erase_page(p) removes facts in <1 ms with 0.00e+00 residual.

M_t ← M_t - γ^Δt · ΔM_doc, (k̄, v̄, z) ← (k̄, v̄, z) - γ^Δt · (Δk̄, Δv̄, Δz)
Chapter 05 · Step 03

Constant-RAM StreamKV Streaming

With --stream-window 8192, Softmax anchors keep 4 float64-rotated sinks + a fixed ring buffer—capping 1.5B RAM at 73.1 MB forever (+0.0 MB growth over 32k+ tokens at 125.6 tok/s).
